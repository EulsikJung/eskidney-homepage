Hemodialysis Center

A nephrologist-run artificial kidney center in Incheon, with an isolation dialysis room

Our hemodialysis center is run directly by nephrology specialists. Whether you are starting dialysis for the first time or transferring from another center, our team helps you continue treatment safely.

What we offer

  • Hemodialysis managed by nephrology specialists
  • Isolation dialysis room for patients with hepatitis B and other infection risks
  • Modern dialysis machines (FMC, Nipro) and water purification
  • Comfortable treatment environment and extended operating hours

Transferring from another center

Yes, you can transfer. Each patient's condition is different, so before deciding to move, please bring your previous dialysis records and visit us. A nephrology specialist will review them and help you continue dialysis safely.
