JES Clinic — Nephrology & Internal Medicine

A nephrologist-run clinic with a hemodialysis center in Ganseok-ogeori, Incheon. International patients welcome.

JES Clinic (정을식내과의원, Jeong Eul-sik Internal Medicine Clinic) is a local clinic in Namdong-gu, Incheon, next to Ganseok-ogeori Station (Incheon Subway Line 1). Our team includes two nephrology specialists along with internal medicine and family medicine doctors — four physicians in total.

We provide hemodialysis in our on-site artificial kidney center, sedated gastroscopy and colonoscopy, national health checkups and cancer screening, and treatment of chronic conditions such as diabetes, high blood pressure, and cholesterol.

Our Services

Hemodialysis

On-site artificial kidney center run by nephrology specialists. Isolation dialysis room available.

Endoscopy

Sedated (painless) gastroscopy and colonoscopy. Same-day gastroscopy possible when fasting.

Health Checkup

National health checkups and cancer screening. Government-designated screening center.

Internal Medicine

Diabetes, hypertension, cholesterol, thyroid, and general internal medicine.
