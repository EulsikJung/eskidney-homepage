Health Checkup & Endoscopy

National checkups, cancer screening, and sedated endoscopy — with same-day results explained by a doctor

JES Clinic is a government-designated health screening center. On the day of your checkup, a doctor explains your results directly, and any abnormal findings are connected to further tests and treatment.

Checkups we provide

  • National health checkup (covered by National Health Insurance)
  • National cancer screening — stomach, colon, liver, breast, cervical cancer
  • Sedated gastroscopy & colonoscopy (painless endoscopy)
  • Blood tests, abdominal ultrasound, and personalized checkups

Endoscopy

We perform sedated (painless) gastroscopy and colonoscopy using FUJIFILM ELUXEO 6000 high-definition equipment. Gastroscopy can often be done the same day if you are fasting; colonoscopy requires bowel preparation, so it is scheduled by appointment.

Results

Items available the same day are explained immediately by a doctor. The full set of results is usually ready within about two weeks.

International patients are welcome. Eligibility for national checkups depends on your insurance status. Please call 032-426-2301 or contact us on KakaoTalk and we will guide you.
